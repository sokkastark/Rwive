# Rwive Design Language v1

This document defines the visual guidelines, core metaphor, and design principles to keep Rwive aligned with its identity: **a personal life companion** that creates perspective, clarity, and long-term awareness.

---

## 🏔️ Core Metaphor
**A personal journey through life, projects, relationships, and growth.** 

Rather than viewing commitments as "tasks" and "deadlines" in a database, Rwive treats them as paths, trails, and milestones on a mountain terrain. The user's focus is on steady momentum (taking steps forward) and long-term reflection (standing on the peak to see where they've been and where they're going).

---

## 🎨 Visual Mood
**Mountain horizons at sunrise.**
* Calm, quiet, and spacious.
* Subtle fog layer gradients.
* Elevation and layered depth.
* Warm sunrise accents.
* Generous negative space and breathing room.

> [!WARNING]
> Do NOT use literal travel illustrations or massive mountain photos. Use mountains as a **mood** (fog gradients, horizon lines, layered depth, typography weight, sunrise highlights) rather than literal decoration.

---

## 📐 Design Principles

* **Calm over Exciting**: Deep slates, fog blues, and soft light. No neon glows, hyperactive animations, or heavy purple startup themes.
* **Perspective over Productivity**: Focus on progress over time, reflection, and continuity, not task velocity or productivity gamification.
* **Journey over Tasks**: Frame activities as steps, milestones, and trails.
* **Awareness over Notifications**: Quietly present what matters most, surrounding it with spacious breathing room.
* **Companion over Dashboard**: Hide details behind toggles, focus the main view on dialogue, and support the user conversationally.
* **Depth over Density**: Show less information, but present what matters more deeply.

---

## 💼 Color Language

* **Slate (Stability)**: Used for main backgrounds and boundaries.
  - `#0b111e` to `#0f172a` (Deep Slate-Blue gradient background)
* **Fog (Uncertainty & Depth)**: Used for cards, borders, and subtext.
  - `#334155` to `#475569` (Fog Gray/Blue borders and card overlays)
* **Sunrise Gold (Focus & Progress)**: Used for active highlights, focus suggestions, and streaks.
  - `#f59e0b` or `#fbbf24` (Soft Gold accent)
* **Snow (Clarity & Light)**: Used for primary text and highlights.
  - `#e2e8f0` to `#f8fafc` (Off-white reading text)

---

## ✍️ Typography & Styling Rules

* **Weight**: Move away from `font-black` and `font-extrabold`. Use `font-light` (300) and `font-normal` (400) for headers.
* **Breathing Room**: Add generous padding (`p-6` to `p-8`) and wide tracking (`tracking-wide` or `tracking-widest`).
* **Minimalist Shadows**: Use subtle, large-radius dark blurs instead of sharp card shadows or glowing borders.

---

## 🗣️ UI & Copywriting Behavior

* **Speak Softly**: Keep dialog clear, gentle, and brief.
* **Observe, Never Judge**: Use objective facts (e.g. *"Film Project has been inactive for 62 days."*) instead of judgmental/emotional phrasing (e.g. *"You have neglected Film Project"*).
* **Guide, Never Pressure**: Offer constructive suggestions (e.g., *"A small step today could restore momentum."*) rather than demanding immediate tasks.
* **Humanize Labels**:
  - `Project Health` ➔ `Trail Status` (*On Trail*, *Losing Momentum*, *Trail Quiet*)
  - `Timeline/Events` ➔ `Trail Log` or `Recent Steps`
  - `Relationships` ➔ `People` or `Connections`
  - `Event Count` ➔ `milestones`
  - `Relationship Count` ➔ `connections`
