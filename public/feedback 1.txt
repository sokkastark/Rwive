This is where your original idea becomes interesting.

There are actually two kinds of intelligence in Rwive.

Intelligence 1: Deterministic Companion (No AI)

Example:

Stark:
Fix sister's scooty in one hour.

Rwive stores:

{
  "type": "commitment",
  "title": "Fix sister's scooty",
  "dueAt": "2026-06-06T17:00"
}

At 5 PM:

Did you fix your sister's scooty?

No AI required.

Just rules.

Same:

Drink ash gourd juice daily.

Next morning:

How was the ash gourd juice today?

Again no AI.

Just habit tracking.

This is where Gemini/OpenAI enters.

Stark:
Need to prepare for appraisal.

AI understands:

{
  "type": "project",
  "category": "career",
  "urgency": "medium"
}

Rwive sees:

Appraisal

Last activity:
9 days ago

Rules detect inactivity.

Then AI generates:

You mentioned your appraisal preparation last week.

Since then there hasn't been any progress recorded.

Would spending 15 minutes reviewing your achievements today help restart momentum?

Now it feels human.
